DES — DynamIQ website assistant · widget prototype v1

Front-end only. The AI is mocked with keyword answers so the flows can be reviewed without a backend. Styling is the live site's .dq-chat widget, unchanged; additions are in widget.css.

Try these

  1. Onboarding in the chat: open the widget and type a question (e.g. Do you handle BIR CAS?). DES asks for consent → name → email/mobile → optional updates opt-in, then answers your original question.
  2. Filipino: start with Magkano ang SAP? — the whole script switches to Filipino.
  3. Layman answers + lead discovery: ask about payroll, Shopee, barcode, integration, or "which product fits". Watch the lead object fill on the right.
  4. No pricing: ask how much — DES redirects to a free business analysis.
  5. Any issue → consultant: type /fail to simulate an LLM error, or tap 👎 on two answers in a row.
  6. Survey: after two answers, click the header ×, or type thanks, bye. Rating 1–5 → reasons → "did you find what you came for".
  7. Returning visitor: reload — DES remembers you for 30 days. Header shows Chatting as … · Not you?

Not in this prototype

  • Real LLM, retrieval and tools (see docs/architecture.md). api.* in widget.js is the swap point.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile on the I agree tap, rate limits, server-side validation — backend concerns.
  • Final copy sign-off (privacy notice text, DPO email placeholder dpo@dynamiqes.com).

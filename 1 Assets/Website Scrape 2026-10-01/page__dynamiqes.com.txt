SAP Business One is a full-scale and highly functional ERP system designed specifically for small businesses up to small and mid-size enterprises. It is an enterprise management system that works on a centralized database that connects all of your business units and employees through a single application.

With SAP B1’s completely integrated functionality, you can have a 360-degree view of all your business functions, a much-streamlined flow of key processes, greater control and sharper insight over and into your business, and more precise decision-making based on real-time information. As a result, the corporate performance of the entire organization is utilized to the fullest towards driving profitable growth.

## SAP BIR CAS Accreditation Assistance

In the Philippines, large taxpayers are required by the Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) to have a BIR-Certified Computerized Accounting System.

A Computerized Accounting System (CAS) combines various component systems to generate computerized accounting books and records that meet government regulations.

Here at DynamIQ, we can help you not only set up your BIR CAS, but we can also help you apply and arrange the necessary requirements with the BIR. Once approved, you’ll be able to enjoy this system’s many features including:

- A streamlined accounting process

- Online viewing of tax transaction

- Business process flow

- Creation of blank database

- Alignment of forms to BIR

- BIR mock demo

- BIR actual presentation assistance

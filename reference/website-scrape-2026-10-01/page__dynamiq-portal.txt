## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Who can use the DynamIQ Portal?

The portal is designed for authorized users within your organization, such as business owners, managers, and team members who need real-time access to business data. Permissions can be customized based on user roles.

What kind of data can I access through the DynamIQ Portal?

You can access all business data in your SAP Business One system, including sales, purchasing, inventory levels, approvals, and more. What data you can see through the DynamIQ portal will be based on your account’s access rights.

How secure is the DynamIQ Portal?

Security is a top priority. The portal uses end-to-end encryption and IQLicense to ensure secure and client-only access to protect sensitive business data.

Can I use the DynamIQ Portal on mobile devices?

Yes. The portal is fully responsive and optimized for desktops, tablets, and smartphones, allowing you to manage your business operations on the go.

Is training required to use the DynamIQ Portal?

The portal is designed to be intuitive and easy to navigate. However, the DynamIQ team is ready to assist and provide training and/or user guides to help your organization and/or new users get started quickly and confidently.

How often is the data updated in the DynamIQ Portal?

The portal provides real-time or near real-time data, depending on your system configuration. This ensures you always have access to the most current information available.

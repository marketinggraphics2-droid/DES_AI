## What is a Barcode Inventory System?

This is a type of software that businesses use to track inventory with the help of barcode technology. In this system, every item is assigned a unique barcode that can be scanned either with a handheld scanner or a mobile device.

This system stores information about the item. When scanned, it is automatically updated in real-time to provide accurate and up-to-date information on your inventory.

## Main Features of a Barcode Inventory System

A barcode inventory system uses barcode scanning technology to streamline how businesses track, manage, and record inventory. Below are its key features designed to improve accuracy, reduce manual work, and enhance operational efficiency.

## Barcode and QR Code Support

Assigns and prints barcodes for individual items and their packaging, making it easier to trace products throughout the supply chain. This ensures that every asset is properly tagged from entry to exit, helping reduce misplacements and delays.

## Batch & Serial Number Barcode

Enables granular tracking by batch or serial number. This feature in barcode inventory systems in the Philippines supports warranty claims, product recalls, and lifecycle tracking, giving your team better oversight over goods tied to specific production runs.

## Product and Location Barcodes

Separates and tags each product and its storage location for streamlined warehouse organization. It simplifies stock retrieval and makes it easier to manage inventory across multiple shelves, zones, or facilities.

## Document Barcode

Barcodes can be printed on operational documents like receipts, invoices, or packing slips. This streamlines record-keeping, speeds up file retrieval, and supports clearer audit trails between departments.

## ID Card

Generates ID cards embedded with barcodes to help verify personnel access and track user activity. This feature adds a layer of security while simplifying identification in high-traffic or restricted areas.

Supports both linear and 2D barcode formats, giving you flexibility in how information is stored and displayed. Compact barcode types allow more data per label, improving scanning accuracy and minimizing clutter.

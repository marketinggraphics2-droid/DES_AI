## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does the DynamIQ BIR Tax Module improve tax compliance compared to manual methods?

Manual tax filing is prone to human error, missed deadlines, and data inconsistencies. The DynamIQ BIR Tax Module automates form generation and reporting directly from SAP Business One data, significantly reducing filing time and increasing accuracy—ensuring compliance with the BIR’s digital mandates.

How does the BIR Tax Module integrate with SAP Business One?

The module pulls financial and transactional data directly from SAP Business One, enabling real-time generation of tax forms, reports, and books of accounts without manual data entry.

Can this module help us eliminate duplicate data entry between our accounting system and BIR applications?

Yes. Since the module integrates natively with SAP Business One, it removes the need to manually re-enter data into standalone applications like eBIRForms. This improves workflow efficiency and ensures your tax forms always reflect your actual books.

Is the module regularly updated to follow changes in BIR regulations or form revisions?

Absolutely. Our team actively monitors BIR announcements and updates the module as needed to ensure continuous compliance. You’ll receive notifications for any critical updates and patches.

Can the BIR Tax Module be customized for my business needs and industry-specific requirements?

Yes. Whether you're a growing SME or a large enterprise, the module can be configured to match your operations, volume of transactions, and industry-specific tax filing needs.

Is user training or support available?

Yes. You’ll receive full post-implementation support, including troubleshooting assistance, module updates, and user guidance. We also offer refresher training sessions upon request.

How long does implementing the BIR Tax Module usually take?

Implementation timelines vary depending on your current SAP Business One setup and specific business requirements. However, most installations with standard configurations can be completed in a few days—including testing and user training.

Can we export the data and reports generated by the module for internal audits or external use?

Yes. All generated forms, journals, and reports can be exported in commonly used formats (e.g., PDF, Excel, DAT), making it easy to support audits, financial reviews, and document submissions.

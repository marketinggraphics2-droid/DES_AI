## What are the minimum hardware or system requirements to run this software?

In general, SAP B1 runs on a Windows-based environment and requires the following minimum specifications:

- Processor: Intel® Xeon® E3 or equivalent

- Memory (RAM): At least 4 GB (16–32 GB recommended for optimal performance)

- Hard Disk: Minimum of 32 GB for the system partition and 10–15 GB for the data partition

- Operating System: 64-bit versions of supported Windows Server editions

- Network: Stable, high-speed internet connection with low latency

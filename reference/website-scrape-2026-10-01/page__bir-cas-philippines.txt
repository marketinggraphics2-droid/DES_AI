## What is a BIR CAS?

A Computerized Accounting System (CAS) integrates different components to produce digital accounting books and computer‑generated records. BIR-certified means that those records meet government requirements.

With these computer-generated documents, businesses of any size can improve their efficiency in data handling and finance procession. Through this digital system, a company’s accounting data can be made available to the required personnel, making sure that important data is always protected. This way, there are fewer risks involved that may stem from manual processing, analyzing, and reporting.

In the Philippines specifically, the Bureau of Internal Revenue requires large taxpayers to use certified systems. These taxpayers are classified under criteria provided by Revenue Regulations No. 1‑1998 and are duly notified by the Commissioner of Internal Revenue.

## Initial Application to Adopt CAS

At the beginning of the application process, you’ll need to prepare and submit the following documents:

These must be filed in the revenue district office that has jurisdiction over your head office. A demonstration of the CAS system will then be conducted. A permit will be given to you after the Computerized System Evaluation Team of the BIR has evaluated and recommended you for approval.

- BIR form 1900 – Application for Authority to Adopt CAS

- Photocopy of BIR Certificate of Registration

- Photocopy of previously Issued Permit (if applicable)

- Photocopy of Current Registration Permit

- Location MAP of the Business

- Inventory of the previously approved unused

- invoices, and receipts (if applicable) List of Branches that will use CAS

## Application for System Enhancement or Modification

A business must also seek the approval of a system enhancement and modification of their BIR CAS in the Philippines. To do this, you must provide them with the following requirements:

- Application Name and Software Used (Development & Database)

- Functions and Features of the Application

- System Flow/s

- Process Flow

- Back-up Procedure, Disaster, and Recovery Plan

- Sworn Statement and Proof of System Ownership

- List of Reports and Correspondences that can be generated from the system with their description, purpose, and sample layout

- Facsimile of System Generated Loose-leaf Books of Accounts and List thereof/Receipts/Invoices

- Additional requirements in case of affiliated companies/sister companies, franchisees, and branches:

- Photocopy of a previously issued permit for mother/sister company or another branch using the same system, if applicable

- Certification from the Computerized System Evaluation Team (CSET), which previously evaluated the approved system

- Photocopy of the previously issued Permit to Adopt CAS or Components Thereof; and

- Letter to the BIR detailing the enhancement/modification to be made on the previously approved CAS or Components Thereof

## Who is required to have a BIR CAS in the Philippines?

Businesses classified as Large Taxpayers (LTs) by the BIR are required to use a CAS. Non-Large Taxpayers may also register a CAS, especially if they choose to issue electronic invoices or automate financial processes. In most cases, companies with complex operations or high transaction volumes are expected to adopt one.

## Is BIR CAS only for large enterprises?

No. While required for Large Taxpayers, businesses of any size using computerized accounting systems—like SAP Business One—may also need to register a CAS to stay compliant with BIR regulations.

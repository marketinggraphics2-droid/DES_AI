## Job Responsibilities

- Lead and manage the implementation projects and assign tasks to functional, technical, and development team members.

- Support agreed-upon scope of work and provide task and time details.

- Monitor and control project activities.

- Ensure resolution of all customer complaints and/or escalations relating to functional, development, and technical services rendered by the business units.

- Assist with the documentation of project implementation plan, business blueprint, user manuals, and functional and technical specifications, in line with client requirements.

- Leads and supports implementation projects, specifically with regards to customized reports

designing and development through crystal reports and add-on development integrated into SAP.

## Qualifications

- Must have 4-5 years of experience as SAP Business One Functional Consultant

- Must possess Bachelor’s/College Degree in Accountancy, Finance, or any relevant courses

- Must have knowledge in taxation and accounting entries

- Knowledgeable and experienced in the technical aspects of software testing

- Good problem-solving and communication skills

- Must be willing to work ONSITE

- Willing to travel and be deployed all over the country.
